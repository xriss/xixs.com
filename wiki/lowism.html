Lowism

Lowism is about doing more with less and embracing the old tech style restrictions willingly in the limitless age. We are creating something as simple as aesthetically possible but no simpler. We are choosing the restrictions so we must choose good restrictions.

I need a name as I have to call this something and low color / low resolution / low poly are how people would generally describe the result so Low is the key word and lo, for lowism has been chosen, call me low for short, low art for fun, lowist to confuse.

There is also low code but that means something totally different. My exposure to using scratch, from the local coder dojo, is one of the reasons I started building Fun64 in the first place.

I wanted to just say minimalist or minimalism even reductionist but that all brings a bunch of baggage nowhere near where I want to be. Lowism kinda ties into lowbrow just by verbal similarity which is honestly closer to anything I'm gonna produce than minimalism is. Same with verbal similarity to taoism/daoism which gives a viable excuse to sneak a cheeky zen slap in every now and again. Not that I need one.

Lowism is the opposite of your magnum opus attempt at a first game project. Lowism is the opposite to limitless miles of AI generated art. Lowism is not a pixel filter applied to a 20mb photograph. Lowism is just five pixels perfectly chosen for you.
