Swanky Paint

Swanky Paint is on Steam.

Swanky Paint is very much a tribute to Deluxe Paint.

Deluxe Paint was the definitive pixel art tool used throughout the late 80s and early 90s to produce art for games and birthed multiple clones providing the same workflow for artists.

It should be understood that DP did not want to be a pixel art tool. Chonky pixels and low colors where just all we had at the time. DP wanted to be an image editing and painting tool and the whole pixel artists thing was more of an accident.

With that in mind please see Lowism for my attempt at explaining exactly why I still need a DP clone some 40 years into the future.

swanky-paint.dp.jpg

I personally used Deluxe Paint on the Amiga until switching to Brilliance around 94. As I migrated to PC I had a brief back track to DOS Deluxe Paint before windows and Cosmigo ProMotion became available in the late 90s. I continued to use ProMotion well into the 2000s and only stoped when I finally jumped ship to Linux.

swanky-paint.bril.jpg

Unfortunately there was no Art Package on Linux that scratched my deluxe itch so I gave up waiting and just wrote my own damn paint package, with blackjack and hookers and a text editor.

swanky-paint.logo.jpg

I should mention that in the early 90s I also worked on Photogenics, a 24bit painting package. Photogenics was bundled with the Amiga in the Magic Pack.

At this point I suspect I may be overly committed to the bit.
